About the channel

Two people, one table, a lot of cardboard

GAMEDEMIC started in 2021 as a way to keep track of the games our group played. Five years later it is a bilingual channel with a shelf that no longer closes and a community that argues about scoring rules in the comments.

We buy most of the games ourselves, mark every sponsored episode and never review a title we have played fewer than three times.

“Read the rules once, watch us play once — and you are ready to teach your own table.”

— the GAMEDEMIC crew
